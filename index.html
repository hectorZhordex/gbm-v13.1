Because spend caps are on by default, your project won't rack up extra charges automatically. You'd need to deliberately toggle spend caps off in your dashboard to allow pay-as-you-grow billing beyond your plan's limits.
Paid plans are charged up front on a monthly cycle. Any usage beyond your included limits is calculated and billed at the end of that billing month.
Where local law requires it, we apply sales tax, VAT, GST, or other indirect taxes based on the billing address on your account. See our billing documentation for the specifics that apply to your region.
Pricing could evolve as the platform grows, but we aim to keep it as fair and developer-friendly as possible, and we'll always give plenty of notice before anything changes.
